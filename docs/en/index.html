About

Integrated solutions to complex challenges - this principle guides all of my professional work. After roughly a decade in teaching and education, I served for many years at the Israeli Prime Minister's Office as an intelligence analyst and manager. Since 2020, I have focused on technology consulting and management. Among other things, I led an antisemitism monitoring project for several months, provided business intelligence services to various clients, and in 2024 founded AvantGuard Eco-Technological Solutions as Co-Founder and CEO, driven by the belief that advanced technology can bridge human, economic and environmental needs. I am fluent in Hebrew, English and German.
